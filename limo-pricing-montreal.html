Montreal Limo Pricing FAQ

Is the price I am quoted the price I pay?

Yes. Your price is fixed and confirmed when you book — that is the price you pay. We do not use surge pricing, and there are no hidden fees added afterwards.

What is a minimum trip charge?

It is the lowest fare for a trip in that vehicle, whatever the distance. Short rides — across downtown, for example — are billed at the vehicle's minimum (from $150, depending on the vehicle) rather than by distance. Your booking always shows the exact fixed price before you confirm.

Why is there a 3-hour minimum for hourly service?

Hourly service reserves the vehicle and chauffeur exclusively for you, so it is booked in blocks starting at 3 hours. For a single trip from A to B, point-to-point fixed pricing is usually the better value — the booking page shows you both.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting time after your flight lands, plus meet & greet inside the terminal. We track your flight and adjust the pickup to your actual arrival.

How do I get an exact price for my trip?

Book online: enter your pickup and destination and the booking page prices every vehicle instantly. Prefer to talk it through? Call or WhatsApp us and we will confirm your fixed price on the spot.
