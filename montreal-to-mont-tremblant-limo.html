Montreal to Mont-Tremblant Limo FAQ

How long does the drive from Montreal to Mont-Tremblant take?

About 2 hours — roughly 140 km — in normal conditions via Autoroute 15 North. Snow, storms, and holiday traffic can add time, so we plan winter pickups with a buffer.

What is the fixed price from Montreal to Mont-Tremblant?

Private transfers start from $650 one-way. Your exact fixed price is confirmed when you book, based on your vehicle and your exact addresses — no surge pricing and no hidden fees.

Can you pick us up at Montreal airport (YUL) and drive straight to Mont-Tremblant?

Yes — it is one of our most popular trips. Airport pickups include meet and greet inside the terminal and 60 minutes of free waiting time after your flight lands.

Is there room for skis, snowboards, and luggage?

Yes. The Cadillac Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, and GMC Yukon Denali have generous cargo space for ski gear and bags. For groups with lots of equipment, the Mercedes Sprinter carries 11 passengers and 12 bags.

Can we book a return trip, or keep the chauffeur for the day?

Absolutely. Book the round trip together, or keep your vehicle by the hour (3-hour minimum) for a full day in the Laurentians — the village, the spa, and dinner before the ride home.
