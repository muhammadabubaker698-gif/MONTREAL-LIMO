All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). Prices shown online before confirmation are estimates; the fare stated in your confirmation email is the final fare. GST (5%) and QST (9.975%) are calculated and itemized at checkout.

Per-vehicle minimum trip charges apply to every booking, regardless of distance: Cadillac Escalade $250; GMC Yukon Denali and Chevrolet Suburban $195 each; Infiniti QX60 and Tesla Model Y $150 each; Mercedes Sprinter $450.

Hourly (as-directed) service has a 3-hour minimum for every vehicle. Hourly time runs from the scheduled pickup time to the final drop-off. Time beyond the booked hours is billed in 30-minute increments at the vehicle's hourly rate.

5. Cancellations — all confirmed bookings are non-refundable

All payments for confirmed bookings are non-refundable. If you cancel a confirmed booking — in whole or in part, for any reason, and however far in advance — no refund will be issued.

If you withdraw a booking request before we have confirmed it, any payment you made will be refunded in full. If we cancel a confirmed trip and cannot offer a comparable alternative, you will receive a full refund of the fare for that trip, which is your sole remedy.

6. Changes and rescheduling

Changes to a confirmed booking (date, time, addresses, vehicle) must be requested at least 48 hours before the scheduled pickup. A change is effective only when we confirm it by email. One change may be accommodated, subject to availability, and any fare difference is payable at the time of the change.

A change requested less than 48 hours before pickup is treated as a cancellation of the original booking (non-refundable under section 5) and a new booking.

7. No-shows and late arrival

If you or your passengers are not at the pickup location and reachable within the grace period — 15 minutes for regular pickups, and 60 minutes after flight landing for airport pickups where a flight number was provided — the trip is recorded as a no-show. No-shows are charged the full fare and are non-refundable, and the chauffeur may leave.

If you are running late, call us at +1 (514) 224-8252 as early as possible; we will try to hold the vehicle, and extra waiting time is billed under section 8.

8. Waiting time

Airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting time after your flight lands. All other pickups include 15 minutes of free waiting time. Waiting beyond the free allowance is billed in 15-minute increments at a pro-rated share of the vehicle's hourly rate and is payable for the trip.
