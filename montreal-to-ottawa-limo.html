Montreal to Ottawa Limo FAQ

How long is the drive from Montreal to Ottawa?

About 2 hours, roughly 200 km, mainly on Autoroute 40 West and Highway 417. Rush-hour traffic leaving either city can add time, and we plan around it for time-sensitive trips.

How much does a Montreal to Ottawa limo cost?

Fixed rates start from $850 one-way and are confirmed in full when you book — no surge pricing and no hidden fees. If you want the chauffeur to wait in Ottawa, hourly service is available instead.

Can I be picked up very early for a morning meeting in Ottawa?

Yes. We operate 24/7 and regularly run early departures timed to arrive for 8 or 9 a.m. meetings in the capital.

Do you offer accounts and invoicing for companies?

Yes — corporate accounts with monthly invoicing and priority dispatch are available for firms that travel between Montreal and Ottawa regularly.

Can the trip start or end at an airport?

Yes. We serve both Montreal (YUL) and Ottawa (YOW) airports. Airport pickups include meet and greet and 60 minutes of free waiting time after landing.
