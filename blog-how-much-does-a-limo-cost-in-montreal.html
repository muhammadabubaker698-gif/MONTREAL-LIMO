Minimum trip charges

For a short point-to-point ride, the minimum trip charge for your vehicle applies even if the trip is only a few kilometres — that is the lowest fare the list above shows in the right-hand column. Your booking always shows the exact total before you pay.

Fixed route prices

Long-distance trips are priced as fixed routes, confirmed before you book:

  • Montreal to Mont-Tremblant — from $460
  • Montreal to Ottawa — from $750
  • Montreal to Quebec City — from $1,030
  • Montreal to Toronto — from $1,760
  • Montreal to the USA (Burlington, Boston, New York) — from $1,200

What can change the price

  • Distance and time beyond the standard route — long one-way trips include the chauffeur’s drive back in the quote, calculated fairly and shown up front.
  • A flat $20.70 airport fee when your pickup is at an airport.
  • Taxes (GST/QST) are shown in your booking total — no surprises at the door, and no forced gratuity added in fine print.

The short answer

A limo in Montreal costs from $100/hr for a Tesla Model Y or Infiniti QX60, $125–$150/hr for a full-size SUV, with fixed prices for airport and long-distance trips. Get your exact number in under a minute in the online booking, or compare everything on our Montreal limo pricing page.
