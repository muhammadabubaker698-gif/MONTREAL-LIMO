Montreal to Quebec City Limo FAQ

How long is the drive from Montreal to Quebec City?

About 3 hours, roughly 250 km, via Autoroute 20 or Autoroute 40 along the St. Lawrence River. Winter weather can add time; we plan pickups accordingly.

What does the Montreal to Quebec City limo cost?

Fixed rates start from $950 one-way and are confirmed in full when you book — no surge pricing and no hidden fees. The price covers the vehicle and chauffeur for the trip.

Can you drop us off inside Old Quebec?

Yes. We serve hotels and addresses throughout Old Quebec and greater Quebec City, including the cruise port terminals and the Convention Centre.

Can we stop along the way?

Short stops — Trois-Rivières, for example — can be built into your booking; just mention them when you reserve. For longer sightseeing stops, hourly service is usually the better fit.

Do you do same-day return trips to Quebec City?

Yes. Your chauffeur can wait while you attend your event or explore the city, then drive you back to Montreal the same day — ask for the hourly option when booking.
