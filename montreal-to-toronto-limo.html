Limo or plane? An honest answer

If you are one person travelling light and every minute counts, a flight can still win on pure travel time. The limo wins for groups, for heavy or awkward luggage, for exact addresses at both ends, for very early or late departures, and for travellers who simply want the trip to be part of the rest, not part of the stress. If your group is heading to a game, a wedding, or a family weekend, it is usually not even close. For recurring corporate travel on this corridor, see our corporate accounts.

Montreal to Toronto Limo FAQ

How long does the Montreal to Toronto drive take?

Roughly 5.5 hours — about 540 km on Highway 401 — including a scheduled rest stop. Traffic around the GTA can add time, especially at rush hour.

What does a Montreal to Toronto limo cost?

Fixed rates start from $1,950 one-way, confirmed at booking with no hidden fees. That is the price for the whole vehicle, not per person, which is why it compares so well for groups.

Is it worth driving instead of flying to Toronto?

For groups, families with lots of luggage, and anyone travelling address-to-address, yes: you skip check-in, security, and transfers at both ends, and everyone rides together. Solo travellers in a hurry may still prefer to fly.

Which Toronto-area destinations do you serve?

Downtown Toronto, Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Mississauga, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough, Vaughan, Markham, and other GTA addresses by reservation.

Can we book a return trip?

Yes — same-day and multi-day returns can be arranged, or keep the vehicle by the hour while you are in Toronto. Book the return at the same time to lock your chauffeur in.
