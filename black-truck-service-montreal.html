What “black truck” actually means

These SUVs are built on truck frames, finished in black, and worked as chauffeur vehicles — that is where the name comes from. When Montrealers ask for a black truck, they want the space and presence of a full-size SUV with a professional driver: six seats, a tall ride, and a cargo area that swallows luggage, ski gear or production cases.

It is not a rental and it is not a taxi. It is a private black SUV service with a chauffeur, booked at a fixed price — the same standard as our corporate chauffeur work, in the biggest vehicles we run.

Black Truck Service Montreal FAQ

What is a black truck service?

It is a chauffeur service built around full-size black SUVs — in our fleet, the Cadillac Escalade ESV, the Chevrolet Suburban and the GMC Yukon Denali. You book the vehicle and a professional chauffeur drives you: airport runs, nights out, corporate travel, or by the hour. They are called black trucks because these SUVs are built on truck frames — it is the same idea as a black car service, with a bigger vehicle.

How much does a black truck service cost in Montreal?

By the hour, the Escalade is $150/h and the Suburban and Denali are $125/h, with a 3-hour minimum on hourly bookings. Point-to-point trips carry a minimum trip charge — $250 for the Escalade and $195 for the Suburban and Denali — and popular routes run at fixed prices confirmed before you book. No surge pricing, no hidden fees.

Is a black truck service the same as a black car service?

Yes — same professional chauffeur, same fixed-price booking, same standard of service. Black car is the industry term and usually means a sedan or a standard SUV; a black truck is the full-size SUV version — Escalade, Suburban, Denali — with more seats, more luggage room and a higher ride. If you searched for a black car service or a black SUV service in Montreal, this is the service you were looking for.

Can I book a black truck for the airport?

Yes — Montreal airport (YUL) pickups are one of the most common black truck bookings. Your chauffeur meets you inside the terminal with meet & greet, helps with your luggage, and airport pickups include 60 minutes of free waiting time after your flight lands. The Escalade ESV and Suburban take checked bags, ski gear and strollers without needing a second vehicle.

How many passengers fit in a black truck?

Six passengers in each of the full-size SUVs — Escalade ESV, Suburban and Denali — and six in the mid-size Infiniti QX60. For 7 to 11 passengers, our Mercedes Sprinter carries the whole group in one cabin, and for larger events we run several black trucks together.
