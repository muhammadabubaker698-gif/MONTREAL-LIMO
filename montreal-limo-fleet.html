Montreal Limo Fleet FAQ

Can I book a specific vehicle?

Yes — that is how we prefer it. Choose your vehicle on the booking page, or use the Reserve button on any vehicle above and it is preselected for you. The vehicle you book is the vehicle that arrives.

Which vehicle is best for a Montreal airport (YUL) pickup?

For most travellers, the Cadillac Escalade ESV: room for six passengers and six bags, and a chauffeur waiting inside the terminal. Groups and ski trips usually take the Suburban or the Sprinter. Airport pickups include meet & greet and 60 minutes of free waiting time.

What is the minimum charge and the hourly minimum?

Every vehicle has a minimum trip charge (shown on its card) that applies to short trips, and hourly service is booked in blocks starting at 3 hours. Full details are on our pricing page.

Do you have a vehicle for more than 11 people?

The Sprinter carries 11 passengers. For larger groups we run multiple vehicles together — weddings, corporate events, and ski clubs do this often. Tell us your headcount and we will plan it.

Are the vehicles shown here the ones that actually arrive?

Yes. The fleet on this page is the fleet we operate — late-model vehicles, cleaned and inspected before every trip, driven by professional chauffeurs.
