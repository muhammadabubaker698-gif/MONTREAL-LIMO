How YUL Airport Limo Pickup Works: A Step-by-Step Guide

Landing at Montréal–Trudeau (YUL) after a long flight, the last thing you want is a taxi line or a surge-priced app ride. Here is exactly how a pre-booked airport limo pickup works with us, step by step.

Step 1: Book before you fly

Book online in a few minutes and choose your vehicle — Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban, GMC Yukon Denali, Infiniti QX60, Tesla Model Y or Mercedes Sprinter for groups. Add your flight number when you book: it is what lets us track your arrival in real time. Your price is a fixed price confirmed before you pay, so the fare you see is the fare you get.

Step 2: We track your flight

Flights rarely land exactly on schedule. We monitor your flight and adjust the pickup time automatically — if you land 40 minutes early or two hours late, your chauffeur is there when you actually walk out, not when the timetable said you would. There is no extra charge for a delayed flight.

Step 3: Land, clear customs, meet your chauffeur

Take your time getting off the plane and through baggage claim: every airport pickup includes 60 minutes of free waiting time after your flight lands. Your chauffeur waits at the arrivals area, and with meet & greet we meet you inside the terminal and walk you straight to the vehicle.

Step 4: Straight to your door

From YUL it is typically 20 to 30 minutes to downtown Montreal depending on traffic, and we also serve Laval, Longueuil, Brossard and the whole Greater Montreal area, plus hotels and private addresses. Your luggage goes in the back, and you are on your way — no shuttle stops, no sharing the ride.

Departing from YUL instead?

The same service works in reverse: we pick you up at home, the office or your hotel and get you to the terminal with time to spare, at any hour — our chauffeurs run 24/7, including early-morning departures and late-night arrivals.

Good to know

  • A flat $20.70 airport fee applies when your pickup is at an airport, shown clearly in your booking total.
  • Groups and extra luggage: the Sprinter carries up to 11 passengers with plenty of room for bags and ski gear.
  • Plans change? Contact us as early as you can and we will do our best to adjust your pickup.
